Hey there!

I'm the co-owner and chief technology officer at Path and Focus, where I build wildfire risk modelling tools. I'm also the founder of Corunio, where I build software for the education and health spaces. This blog is a collection of some experiments in machine learning, software development, and web application security. You can find me on Twitter, Github, LinkedIn, or through a quick email.
